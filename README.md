# Keralam — A Malayalam Watchface for Pebble Time Steel

*കേരളത്തിന്റെ ആത്മാവ്, നിങ്ങളുടെ കൈത്തണ്ടയിൽ.*
*The soul of Kerala, on your wrist.*

---

**Keralam** is a handcrafted watchface for the Pebble Time Steel that brings the beauty of the Malayalam language and Kerala's cultural identity to your wrist. Every detail — from the numerals to the inscription — is a quiet nod to the land of coconut trees, backwaters, and Onam.

## The Clock Face

The upper half of the screen is a clean, rectangular analog clock face rendered in a warm cream e-paper tone. Twelve Malayalam numerals (൧ through ൧൨) sit around the perimeter, evenly spaced across the top and bottom edges with ൩ and ൯ anchoring the sides. The four corner numerals can be rotated 45° to follow the diagonal of each corner — a small typographic flourish borrowed from classic travel clocks. Sixty tick marks ring the face, with the corner hour marks also angled diagonally for visual consistency.

The hands are drawn pixel by pixel. You can choose between a smooth line, chunky pixel blocks, or a tapered pixel style — each giving the face a distinct character.

## The Date Strip

Just below the clock, a single line carries the date in mixed Malayalam and Arabic: day of the week in Malayalam (തിങ്കൾ), followed by the numeric date, month name in Malayalam (മേയ്), and a two-digit year. Compact, readable, and entirely in the language.

## The Numeral Legend

The lower half carries a quiet ghost grid of all twelve Malayalam numerals paired with their Arabic equivalents — a subtle reference panel for anyone learning to read them. It sits at low opacity, present without demanding attention.

## Battery Indicator

You have four ways to display battery status, each reflecting a different aesthetic:

- **Circular Spiral** — a smooth Archimedean spiral that unwinds from the center outward as charge decreases
- **Flower Radial Fill** — an Athapookkalam-inspired ring pattern that fills clockwise, like petals being placed one by one
- **Square Spiral** — a rectangular Aldo-style spiral, crisp and geometric
- **Battery Bar + %** — a classic rectangular bar with a large percentage readout

The battery indicator can live inside the clock face or be moved to the lower half of the screen.

## The Inscription

At the very bottom, in small grey type:

**കേരളം, നവംബർ 1, 1956**

The date Kerala was formed as a state — a permanent, quiet reminder of where this watchface comes from.

## Customisation

Every aspect of the watchface can be tuned from the settings page in the Pebble app:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Background | Cream · White · Light Grey |
| Numeral Weight | Regular · Bold |
| Corner Numerals | Rotated 45° · Flat |
| Hand Style | Smooth · Pixel Blocky · Pixel Tapered |
| Battery Style | Circular Spiral · Flower · Square Spiral · Bar % |
| Battery Position | On Watch Face · Bottom Left |
